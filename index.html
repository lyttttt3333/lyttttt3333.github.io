About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Ping Luo, and a research intern in the Efficient AI team at NVIDIA Research with Enze Xie and Prof. Song Han. I received a dual degree from Tsinghua University: a B.S. in Mathematics and Physics and a B.Eng. in Measurement & Control Technology and Instruments.

My research spans three directions: agents that improve AI systems on their own, together with the systems that make large generative models fast; efficient training and post-training of generative models; and robot learning.
